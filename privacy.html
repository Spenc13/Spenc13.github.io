ALFRED Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Who uses ALFRED

ALFRED is a private personal assistant built and used solely by its owner, Spencer Faircloth. It is not offered to the public, and no other people use it or sign into it.

What ALFRED accesses

With the owner's explicit permission through Google's sign-in process, ALFRED may access the owner's own Google account data, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Contacts, in order to help the owner read, organize, and draft content for his own use.

How data is handled

  • ALFRED runs on the owner's personal computer. Google sign-in credentials are stored only on that computer.
  • To answer the owner's requests, relevant content may be processed by the AI model providers the owner has chosen (such as Anthropic), under those providers' own terms.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than assisting the owner.
  • ALFRED's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke ALFRED's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
