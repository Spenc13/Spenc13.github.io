# title: Remodeler: "too expensive"
# Dana owns a kitchen and bath remodeling company. Interested, but balks at price and asks about month-to-month.
Rep: Hi Dana, Spencer from Olly Olly. Thanks for making time. What got you looking at marketing help this year?
Prospect: We do kitchens and baths, high-end mostly. Our leads have gotten worse. A lot of tire kickers asking for a free quote and then ghosting.
Rep: What does a good lead look like for you?
Prospect: Someone with a budget over forty grand who's ready in the next three months. We close about half of those.
Rep: And how many of those are you getting a month right now?
Prospect: Maybe two or three. We need about six to keep both crews booked.
Rep: What happens if that stays at two or three through next year?
Prospect: I'd have to let one crew go. I don't want to do that, these guys have been with me eight years.
Rep: That makes sense. Based on that, the Strategic plan fits. It's a 10 to 15 page site, social posting twice a week, and retargeting ads, and it runs five hundred to seven fifty a month.
Prospect: Seven fifty a month? That's a lot. That's too expensive for us right now, honestly.
[pause 1]
Prospect: Is there a cheaper option? And can I just do it month to month so I'm not locked in?
Rep: We could probably do month to month.
Prospect: How long until I'd actually see more calls?
Rep: Usually a few months. Let me put a proposal together and send it over.
Prospect: Okay. I'll need to run it by my husband anyway, he does the books.
