# title: Pool service owner: burned by an agency
# Luis runs a pool cleaning and repair company. A past agency took his money and he's skeptical.
Rep: Hey Luis, Spencer with Olly Olly. Thanks for the time. What made you take the call?
Prospect: I'll be honest with you, I almost hung up. We tried an agency two years ago and it was a waste of money.
Rep: It sounds like you got burned before.
Prospect: Yeah. Twelve hundred a month for a year. They sent me reports full of clicks and impressions and the phone didn't ring any more than before.
Rep: What did they actually do for you?
Prospect: Some ads, a couple of blog posts. I never knew who called because of them and who didn't.
Rep: What would you need to see in the first thirty days to know this was working?
Prospect: Actual calls. Names and numbers of people who found us. Not a pretty chart.
Rep: Our tracking reports show who visited, where they came from, and who called or requested a quote.
Prospect: Everybody says that. Can you guarantee more calls?
[pause 1]
Prospect: Spring is my busy season. If I sign up, when can you start? And who else in pools do you work with?
Rep: Great question. We could start next week. Does a quick call Thursday at ten work to go over the setup?
Prospect: Thursday at ten works.
