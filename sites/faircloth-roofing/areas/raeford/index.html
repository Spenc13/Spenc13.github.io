Roofing in Raeford

Raeford is Hoke County's seat and home of the North Carolina Turkey Festival. Much of Hoke's growth comes from Fort Bragg families, so newer subdivisions are a big part of our work here.

Hoke County catches the same tropical systems as Fayetteville, plus summer thunderstorms rolling in from the west.

Whatever brought you here, a leak, a storm, or a roof that's just old, the first step is the same: a free inspection with photos and a straight answer on repair versus replacement.

FAQ

Roofing in Raeford: questions

Don't see yours? Call and a real roofer will answer.

Do you charge a trip fee to Raeford?

No. Raeford is about 20 miles from our Fayetteville shop, inside our 50-mile service area, so inspections are free.

How fast can you get to Raeford after a storm?

Active leaks come first. When water is getting in, we aim to have a tarp on the same day, and full inspections follow within a few days of a big storm. demo

Do you pull permits?

Yes. We pull the permit with whichever office handles your address, the town or Hoke County, and schedule the inspection.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. Active duty, veterans, Guard and Reserve, military spouses and Gold Star families save 12% on a full replacement. demo
