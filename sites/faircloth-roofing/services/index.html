Roofing services

Replacements, storm repair, metal, commercial, leak repair and gutters for homes and businesses across Fayetteville and the Sandhills.

Roof replacement

Full tear-off down to the deck, rotten boards replaced, new underlayment, flashing, vents and shingles. Most homes are done in one to two days.

  • Architectural shingle
  • 3-tab
  • Synthetic slate

Storm & hail repair

Wind-lifted shingles, hail bruising, limbs through the roof. We tarp it the same day and photograph every hit for your insurance claim.

  • Emergency tarping
  • Hail inspections
  • Insurance claims
Learn more

Metal roofing

Standing-seam and screw-down panel for homes, farmhouses, barns and shops. Sheds heavy rain fast and holds up to hurricane gusts.

  • Standing seam
  • R-panel & 5V
  • Metal retrofits
Learn more

Commercial flat roofs

TPO, EPDM, PVC and modified bitumen for offices, warehouses, churches and retail. Repairs, coatings and full replacements.

  • TPO
  • EPDM
  • PVC
  • Coatings
Learn more

Leak repair & inspections

We trace the water to the flashing, pipe boot or valley that's actually failing, then give you a written report with photos.

  • Leak tracing
  • Flashing
  • Real-estate reports
Learn more

Gutters

5" and 6" seamless aluminum formed on site, with downspouts and leaf guards in colors to match your trim.

  • Seamless aluminum
  • Leaf guards
  • Downspouts
