    Storm restoration & insurance claims

    Hurricane season runs June through November. We'll be ready.

    Cumberland County sits in the path of Atlantic hurricanes and spring hail. Matthew and Florence flooded this region in 2016 and 2018. If a storm finds your roof, here's how a claim goes with us.

    1. Free storm inspection

      We walk the roof, gutters, siding and attic and photograph every bit of wind or hail damage.

    2. Emergency protection

      If water is getting in, we tarp it the same day so your policy's "prevent further damage" clause is covered.

    3. You file, we document

      You open the claim with your insurer. We give you a photo report and a line-item scope to send along.

    4. Adjuster meeting

      We meet the adjuster on site and point out everything we found so nothing gets missed.

    5. Build day

      Most homes are finished in one to two days. Magnetic sweeps pick up nails from your yard and driveway.

    6. Close-out

      Final photos, warranty registration and any supplements so you get every dollar your policy owes.

    FAQ

    Questions we hear every week.

    Don't see yours? Call or send a note and a real roofer will answer.

    How do I know if hail damaged my roof?

    From the ground, look for dents on gutters, downspouts and the metal vents on your roof. Those usually mean the shingles took hits too. Hail bruises are hard to spot without getting on the roof, so book a free inspection after any storm with hail bigger than a quarter.

    Do you work with my insurance company?

    Yes. We work with all major carriers. You file the claim, and we provide photos, a line-item scope and meet the adjuster on site. We never ask you to sign over your claim.

    How long does a roof replacement take?

    Most homes take one to two days. Larger, steeper or slate and tile roofs can take a week. Commercial projects are scheduled around your business hours.

    Should I choose shingles or metal?

    Architectural shingles cost less up front and suit most homes. Standing-seam metal costs roughly two to three times more but can last twice as long and handles hurricane winds better. The estimator above shows both side by side.

    Do you repair commercial flat roofs?

    Yes. We repair, coat and replace TPO, EPDM, PVC, modified bitumen and built-up roofs, and offer maintenance plans that keep your manufacturer warranty valid.
